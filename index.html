Paul Tosh

Unhurried, a little outlaw, completely self-possessed.

Where the theme demands it, I supplement the cover material with chosen original songs (and vice versa) — songs that illuminate the same terrain from a different angle. The theatrical handling of cover songs is among my core performing strengths, honed across years of The Song Is The Play. The two bodies of work inform each other. This means there will always be an element of surprise in the running order — the show breathes differently each time. It is a show with both cultural relevance and performance depth.

Show I · Original Operetta

Paper
Gun

This is mine, but you can have it.

One man at the piano, playing both sides of himself. Everyone carries the empath and the narcissist — and when we're deep in one, we notice and react to the other. Built from songs performed over a decade. Not explaining the human condition. Demonstrating it from the inside.

"A three act paper theme tied to the shadow and sacrifice, a journey from disillusionment to authenticity"
