The Build

What follows is the list of adaptations, with thanks to the suppliers who made each one possible. This is probably the most adapted and customized Cybertruck out there.

  • CyberPhoenix camper — mounted with the sub-trunk lid removed and a hidden hatch built in for access  Phoenix Custom Campers
  • Rear seats removed and a custom shelf fitted to carry the live rig  ProFab
  • Lap-crossing velcro work table, also built by ProFab
  • BOSE monitor system for editing on the road
  • All windows tinted with full UV protection  Urban Werks, Lancaster
  • Second Tesla camera — housing at the back of the camper; a hole drilled inside the tailgate reaches the camera plug, switched over and re-calibrated  Zak at 3D Print Master, Manheim
  • Special cables for the Tesla camera repositioning  Pasternack
  • Frunk cushions — a nice shady seat outside
  • White wrap and artwork sponsored by Man Cave, Colorado
  • Additional artwork by Wild Bill  acapfilmspa.com
  • Glass pass-through — precision hole cut in the Cybertruck between bed and cabin (thanks Kaycee!), letting the Breezer vent system lead the truck’s AC into the camper  Lancaster Paint & Glass Co. · Ben at Breezer Windows
  • Back-up AC unit for when the camper comes off the truck during on-site supervision job at Kreider’s, Manheim, PA  Kreider Farms — Lancaster County Fresh
  • Torklift turnbuckles for the camper tie-downs  torklift.com
  • Puddle lights  custompuddlelights.com

Every one of these solved a real problem on the road. Together, they turn the USA into a village.
