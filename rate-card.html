Rate Card

Every figure below is all-inclusive — travel, accommodation and sound are carried by me. I arrive in a self-contained performance camper, so there is no hotel rider and no staging to arrange.

A Night at the Theatre

$2,452

The full operetta in one night. Three acts of thirty-five minutes, two ten-minute breaks and a ten-minute talkback — roughly two and a half hours in the room.

A Two-Part Show at the Theatre

$3,065

The same operetta restructured for two evenings — two acts of fifty minutes, one on each night, with a ten-minute talkback at the end of the second.

An Evening With a Short Show

$1,226

The combo set — a broader introduction to material from the original operetta, Paper Gun, plus Dylan, Diamond, The Beatles and Billy Joel performed as theatrical prose, all with live looping piano. Neapolitan song with new words, stories from the road, and a talkback.

The piano

If you can get a real piano into the room, it is a great deal better. It is not only far more classy to look at — the music itself is exponentially better, and if you do not already have one, it is genuinely worth making that choice.

I can bring a keyboard, for an extra $72. But for a little more than that, the money is better spent on getting a piano into the room. The difference in the performance is disproportionate to the cost.

What I ask of the room

  • A real acoustic piano, where it can be arranged
  • A quiet place to park the camper overnight
  • Hot water at the interval

If possible

  • Somewhere to shower, at any hour
  • A shore-power hook-up — an ordinary outdoor 120V socket is enough
