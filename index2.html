Paper Gun — a show about shadow work

It's about survival over grievance. An original operetta — one man plays both sides of himself. A three-act paper theme tied to the shadow and sacrifice: a journey from disillusionment to authenticity. A niche, unconventional, therapeutic, theatrical one-man show.

An honest word: Paper Gun goes deeper than most entertainment — framed, held, and closed with care — see the therapeutic link below.

I've been introducing these songs of pain and love and reconciliation from my operetta in churches, synagogues, community centers, and senior facilities — I am now touring my one-man operetta; where the theme demands it, I supplement the original material with chosen cover songs by Bob Dylan, Frank Sinatra, Neil Diamond & Billy Joel — songs that illuminate the same terrain from a different angle. The theatrical handling has been my core performing approach, honed across years of The Song Is The Play. There will always be an unknown element — the show breathes differently each time, with relevance, and with a few surprises.
