3 · Technical Requirements

  • A real piano.
  • Protective peelable black soft velcro is applied to the piano to prevent marking when securing performance equipment. After the show you may choose to keep it in place if future performances are anticipated.
  • No eating during the performance — only before, after, or during intermission.

4 · Setup, Breakdown & Performance Times

Setup, soundcheck, and performance start times are scheduled elements of this production.

  • Initial setup: 60 minutes · Sound check: 5 minutes
  • Doors open to audience: at least 30 minutes prior to performance (more is fine)
  • Post-show meet & greet: approx. 20 minutes
  • Additional buffer: 30 minutes pre- or post-show as needed
  • Total on-site time required: approx. 4 hours

5 · Parking

On longer trips I travel in a mobile camper — I’d be grateful if you could provide a safe overnight space the night before a performance: somewhere on the facility grounds, or outside a home in a safe suburban area.

Thank you for your cooperation — looking forward to a memorable event.
